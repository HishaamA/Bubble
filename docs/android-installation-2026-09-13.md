## Precision-first automatic albums and incorrect-match corrections

- Audited the installed Human 3.3.6 matching source: the app's distance normalization matches it (100 synthetic comparisons, no differences). The old automatic acceptance was too permissive: the mesh/detector score is not identity confidence, composite quality can be high for a 20px face, and max-over-reference matching allowed a lone weak appearance to dominate.
- Automatic matches now require 0.92 similarity to one known-quality enrollment, or at least 0.86 with a second qualifying reference at 0.82 and an enrollment anchor at 0.78. Same-photo/near-identical references do not count as corroboration. Automatic quality, detection, pixel-detail and ambiguity checks are separate: quality at least 0.75, detector and mesh scores at least 0.75, face detail at least 80px, person-score margin 0.12 and competing-face margin 0.08. These are conservative application rules, not calibrated identity probabilities or a guarantee of correctness.
- Kept Review selective: only plausible 0.78-or-better matches with quality/detail checks are offered; weaker matches remain untagged. Low-quality or unknown-quality references cannot independently authorize automatic matching. A single manually confirmed appearance without enrollment support cannot silently classify a different identity. Previously rejected learned appearances no longer influence suggestions.
- Fixed a distinct group-photo error: manually confirming an identity used to remove it from the rival score matrix. The remaining face could then be falsely attributed to its next-closest relative. All identities/faces remain competing evidence now; confirmed outputs alone are suppressed. Legacy stored inferred assignments are re-evaluated against the current policy instead of bypassing it. Manual confirmations, scans, dates and originals remain preserved.
- New scans retain bounded `minFacePixels`. Legacy scans use a conservative pixel-size lower bound derived from their recorded quality components; explicit invalid size is an ineligible zero sentinel, not an optimistic fallback. New extraction rejects wrong-length, sparse, non-finite and all-zero FaceRes vectors as failed/retryable output. No model/scan revision bump or forced full-library rescan.
